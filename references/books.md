# Recommended books

- Hecht, E. (2017). *Optics*, 5th edition. Pearson.
- Born, M. and Wolf, E. (1999). *Principles of Optics*, 7th expanded edition. Cambridge University Press.
- Lakowicz, J. R. (2006). *Principles of Fluorescence Spectroscopy*, 3rd edition. Springer. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-0-387-46312-4
- Pawley, J. B., ed. (2006). *Handbook of Biological Confocal Microscopy*, 3rd edition. Springer. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-0-387-45524-2
- Saleh, B. E. A. and Teich, M. C. (2019). *Fundamentals of Photonics*, 3rd edition. Wiley.

Textbook PDFs are not included; purchase, institutional access, or open licensed editions must be used lawfully.
